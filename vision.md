# Infinity Den — product brief

This is the decided version of the product idea. Build from this file, not from
earlier brainstorming. Anything not listed here is out of scope until it is
added here first.

**Display names are provisional.** Room and feature names (Cosmic Canvas,
Question Room, ...) are display text and may change at any time. Slugs, URLs,
and code identifiers are plain and permanent — see "Naming convention" in
`instructions.md`.

---

# POSITIONING

Infinity Den is a community where people discover knowledge, contribute lived
experience, and explore ideas together.

It is not "a website with articles, debates, quizzes, and blogs". Most sites
organize around what happened or what someone wants to publish. Infinity Den
organizes around what people know, what they believe, what they have
experienced, and what they want to understand.

Tagline: *A universe of ideas. A community of minds.*
Supporting line: *Explore what you don't know. Share what you do. Question what you believe.*

---

# THE FOUR PILLARS

| Pillar | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Discover | Explainers, research, forgotten histories, and questions people rarely ask |
| Contribute | Anyone with something meaningful can publish essays, experiences, perspectives, and research |
| Discuss | A respectful space for questions, disagreement, and community responses |
| Play and learn | Short games that turn curiosity into a habit, each linking to deeper reading |

**The pillars must connect.** An article is never a dead end: it leads to a
related question, a perspective, a story, or the next article. Every quiz
answer links to an article that explains it.

# ROOMS (SUBJECT TAXONOMY)

Rooms describe the **subject**. Formats (below) describe **how people engage**.
A personal story about failure belongs to a room *and* to the Human Library.

Every room has a creative name **and** a descriptive subtitle. The subtitle is
always visible — in navigation, on room cards, and in search — so new visitors
never have to guess.

# CONTRIBUTION FORMATS

Members contribute in three formats. Each format carries a visible label so
readers always know what kind of claim they are reading.

| Format | Label shown | For | Expectations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `article` | Researched | Explanations, original analysis, detailed arguments | Sources required for factual claims |
| `experience` | Personal experience | First-person accounts, life lessons, reflections | No credentials needed; not presented as universal evidence |
| `perspective` | Opinion | A short argument, a response, a different interpretation | Clearly an opinion, not a fact |

Any piece can additionally be flagged **speculative** (predictions, open
hypotheses), which adds a "Speculation" label.

Questions are not an article format. In Phase 1 they are curated by editors
(Question Room); member-asked questions come later (Open Question Board).

The editor gives every contribution: title, summary, room, tags, optional cover
image, and source links where relevant.

# FEATURE DESTINATIONS

| Destination | URL | Phase | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question Room | `/questions` | 1 | One thought-provoking question per day. Members respond with a short thought, a longer answer, a personal story, or a researched answer. Editors feature the most insightful responses, not the most popular. |
| Human Library | `/experiences` | 1 | All `experience` contributions, browsable by room. Prompts help people start: "An experience that changed how I think", "Something I wish I had known five years ago", "A mistake that taught me more than success", "A belief I used to hold but no longer do". |
| Curiosity Arcade | `/play` | 1 (Fact or Fiction only) | Fact or Fiction: decide whether a surprising claim is true, then see the explanation and a link to a deeper article. |
| Debate Den | `/debates` | 2 | A motion, arguments for and against, evidence, rebuttals, and "did you change your mind, and why?". Readers rate an argument's clarity, evidence, and reasoning separately from whether they agree. |

# HOMEPAGE

The homepage should feel like entering a world of ideas, not opening a news feed.

In order:

1. **Hero** — brand, tagline, supporting line, two actions: "Explore ideas" and "Share your story".
2. **Today's Question** — the current question and a way to respond.
3. **From the Human Library** — featured personal experiences.
4. **Curiosity Arcade** — one Fact or Fiction claim, answered in place.
5. **Explore by room** — room cards with name and subtitle.
6. **Ideas worth your time** — editor-selected pieces.
7. **From the community** — new voices and recent contributions, including from new members.
8. **Write for Infinity Den** — a prominent invitation to contribute.

Phase 2 adds a featured Debate Den section. On desktop, a sidebar may hold the
daily quiz and trending questions; on mobile these become compact cards.

# COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Decisions that apply from day one:

- Facts, personal experiences, opinions, and speculation are labelled separately.
- Factual claims in `article` contributions require sources; the process stays approachable for new contributors.
- Members can report content; editors can hide it. Blocking comes with member profiles.
- Plagiarism and undisclosed AI-generated material are handled under a published editorial policy.
- Nothing is ranked by popularity alone. Editor picks and new members' contributions get visible space.
- Critique arguments, not people.
- Corrections are easy to make and visible on the corrected piece.

# PHASES

## Phase 1 — The foundation

Goal: valuable even with a small community.

- Launch rooms with subtitles, and room-based navigation
- Member accounts and contributor profiles
- Member publishing (article, experience, perspective) through an editorial review queue
- Question Room with member responses and featured responses
- Human Library
- Curiosity Arcade: Fact or Fiction
- The new homepage and visual identity
- Search, room and format filters, bookmarks, and comments
- Basic moderation: review queue, reports, hide content

## Phase 2 — Interaction

Goal: reasons to return and participate.

- Debate Den with separate clarity / evidence / reasoning ratings
- More Arcade games (One-Minute Quiz, Guess the Connection, Daily Brain Spark)
- Contributor reputation based on useful sources, clear explanations, and thoughtful rebuttals — not follower counts
- Follow rooms and writers
- Perspective Switch ("You've read the case for X — now read the strongest case against")
- Related-idea recommendations

## Phase 3 — Community growth

Goal: a network of connected ideas and contributors.

- Idea Web and Reading Trails
- Open Question Board (member-asked questions)
- Community research projects and curated collections
- My Curiosity Map and personalized discovery
- Live discussions or challenges, if members want them

## Not now

Sponsored content, business listings, and news/guide/review formats belong to
the previous version of the site and are being removed. Also deferred:
Anonymous Story Mode, "Explain it simply", "Unpopular but thoughtful", and
Idea of the Week — revisit after Phase 2.

---

# VOICE AND FEEL

Intellectually curious, slightly mysterious, modern, and human. Not academic,
not childish, not a conventional news portal. The name suggests a place with no
end to what you can explore; navigation and copy should reinforce that sense of
endless discovery — while subtitles keep it clear.
