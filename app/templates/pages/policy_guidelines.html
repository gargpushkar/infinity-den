Infinity Den works when people can share what they know, and disagree about it, without fear. These guidelines apply to everything here: pieces, responses, comments, and profiles.

Critique arguments, not people

Disagree as strongly as you like with an idea. Don't attack, mock, or label the person who holds it. "This argument ignores X" helps everyone. "Only an idiot would think this" helps no one.

Say what kind of claim you're making

Every piece carries a label: Researched, Personal experience, or Opinion, and Speculation when it predicts or guesses. Choose the honest one. A personal experience is worth hearing, but it isn't universal evidence, and an opinion isn't a fact.

Back up facts

Researched pieces need sources for their factual claims. In responses and comments, link a source when you state something others can check.

Share your own story, protect other people's

Your experiences are yours to tell. The people in them didn't choose to be written about: change names and details so they can't be identified, and never share someone's private information.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats, or bullying
  • Hate or demeaning content aimed at people for who they are
  • Sharing someone's private details (addresses, phone numbers, photos, documents) without their permission
  • Sexually explicit content, and any sexual content involving minors
  • Encouraging violence or self-harm
  • Spam, advertising, and self-promotion that isn't part of a genuine contribution
  • Pretending to be someone else
  • Plagiarism, and AI-written pieces passed off as your own (see the editorial policy)
  • Anything illegal

When something breaks the rules

Every piece, response, and comment has a Report link. Reports go to the editors, and the person you report never sees who reported them. Nothing is hidden automatically: an editor reads each report and decides whether to hide the content or leave it up.

Content that breaks these guidelines is hidden. Repeated or serious breaks, such as threats or sharing private details, can lead to an account being closed.

If you think someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.
