Anyone with something meaningful to say can write for Infinity Den. Editors help pieces reach readers in good shape, and keep the labels honest. This page explains how.

How review works

Every piece a member writes is read by an editor before it's published. The editor checks the title, summary, room, tags, cover image, and sources, and then does one of three things:

  • Publishes it, sometimes with small fixes to spelling, formatting, the summary, or tags.
  • Sends it back with a note, when it needs changes. You can edit it and submit it again.
  • Declines it, when it doesn't fit these policies or the community guidelines.

Editors don't change what you argue. If a piece needs more than small fixes, it comes back to you. Once a piece is published it's locked, and changes go through an editor.

Responses in the Question Room and comments on pieces appear straight away. Editors read reports about them and hide anything that breaks the guidelines.

Labels

Every piece shows what kind of claim it makes:

  • Researched: explanation or analysis based on evidence. Factual claims need sources.
  • Personal experience: a first-person account. No credentials needed; not presented as universal evidence.
  • Opinion: an argument or interpretation, clearly not a statement of fact.
  • Speculation: added to any piece that predicts or proposes an open hypothesis.

Editors may change a label that doesn't match the piece, and will tell you why.

Sources

Researched pieces list their sources at the end. Link to the original where you can (the study, the dataset, the primary document) rather than to someone's summary of it.

Your own work

What you submit must be your own. Quote others briefly and credit them. If a piece first appeared somewhere else, you can still share it here if you hold the rights; say where it was first published.

Plagiarism, meaning someone else's words or ideas presented as yours, is removed, and the account that posted it may be closed.

AI tools

You can use AI tools to check spelling and grammar, or to help translate your own writing. If AI wrote a substantial part of a piece, say so in the piece. Editors may decline pieces that are mostly AI-written, and remove undisclosed ones. The same goes for AI-generated cover images: mention it in the piece.

Corrections

If a published piece gets a fact wrong, tell us: use the Report link on the piece (choose "Misinformation" or "Something else") or write to {{ contact_email }}. When an editor corrects a piece, a dated correction note appears at the end saying what was wrong and what changed. We don't change facts silently.

Independence

Infinity Den doesn't publish sponsored content or sell placement. Editor's picks are chosen because a piece is worth your time, and nothing is ranked by popularity alone; new members' work gets visible space too.

Who owns what you write

You do. The terms of use explain the permission you give us to show it.
