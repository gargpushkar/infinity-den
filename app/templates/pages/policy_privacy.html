The short version: we keep only what we need to run the site. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or rent your information.

Infinity Den runs this site. Questions about this policy, or requests about your information, go to {{ contact_email }}. These rights apply to everyone, wherever you live.

What we store

If you join

  • Your email address, to sign you in. It's never shown on the site. We use it to email you about your account (confirming your address, password resets) and when an editor publishes your piece or sends it back. When you join, we email you a link to confirm it; until you do, you can read but not take part, and an account that isn't confirmed within 7 days is deleted.
  • Your username, display name, and bio. These are public on your profile and next to what you publish.
  • Your password, stored only as a secure hash. We can't read it.

What you write

  • Published pieces, responses, debate arguments, and comments are public.
  • Drafts and pieces waiting for review are visible only to you and the editors.
  • Saved pieces are visible only to you.
  • Reports are visible only to the editors. The person you report doesn't see who reported them.

Images you upload

We re-save every uploaded image and remove its hidden details, including the location where a photo was taken and the camera that took it.

Password resets

When you ask to reset your password, we email a link that works once, for an hour. We don't store the link.

The newsletter

If you subscribe, we store your email address and the date you signed up.

Technical information

Like most websites, our server records requests (IP address, browser type, the page asked for, and the time) to keep the site secure and fix problems. These logs are kept for a short time. IP addresses are also held briefly in memory to slow down repeated sign-in attempts.

Cookies

We use only the cookies the site needs to work. None of them are for advertising or tracking.

  • member_access_token: keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days.
  • member_csrf_token: protects forms from being submitted by other websites.
  • theme: remembers whether you chose light or dark mode, for a year.
  • Editors have two more cookies that do the same jobs on the editors' side of the site.

Who else handles information

  • Our hosting provider runs the server the site lives on.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores the site's database.
  • jsDelivr serves the Bootstrap styles and scripts the site is built with. Like any website your browser contacts, it receives your IP address.
  • Images linked from other websites. When a piece uses a cover image linked from another site, your browser loads it from that site.
  • Brevo delivers our emails, such as password-reset links. It receives your email address and the message we send.

We share information with others only when the law requires it, or to protect people's safety.

These services may store and process information in countries other than yours, including the United States and India.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, for as long as you keep it.
  • What you publish, until you or an editor removes it, or your account is deleted.
  • Newsletter addresses, until you unsubscribe. Every newsletter will include a way to unsubscribe, or you can write to us.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or send you a copy. You can change your display name and bio yourself on your account page. To delete your account, use Delete your account at the bottom of your account page. Your account is deactivated straight away, and we delete it, with everything you wrote, within 30 days. Reports you made stay with the editors, without your name. You can also write to {{ contact_email }} from the email address you signed up with, and we'll delete it within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Infinity Den isn't for children under 13. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, the site is served over an encrypted connection, and only editors can see unpublished work and reports. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters, we'll say so on the site.
